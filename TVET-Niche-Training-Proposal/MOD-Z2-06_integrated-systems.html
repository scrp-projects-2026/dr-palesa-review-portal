Commissioning Lifecycle Methodology

Four-Phase Structured Quality Assurance Standard

Phase Lifecycle Stage Key Verification Tasks Sign-off Gate
Phase 1 Pre-Commissioning (Cold) Hydrostatic line tests, point-to-point loop checks, rotation checks, dry stroke testing. Ready for Energization (RFE)

Pre-Commissioning & Cold Loop Testing

Un-energized Mechanical & Electrical Integrity Verification

Mechanical & Piping Line Checks
  • Hydrostatic Testing: Pressurizing loop piping to 1.5× design pressure for 4 hours.
  • Flushing & Cleaning: Water high-velocity flush to clear debris prior to membrane loading.
  • Alignment Verification: Laser alignment of all pump and agitator shaft couplings.
Narrator Insight: Validates mechanical containment strength prior to fluid introduction.
Electrical & I&C Cold Audits
  • Megger Insulation Checks: Motor winding dielectric strength tests.
  • Dry I/O Simulation: Forcing PLC digital and analog points to verify SCADA graphics.
  • Valve Stroke Calibration: Confirming 0–100% actuator response and limit switch feedback.
Narrator Insight: Ensures control logic and electrical insulation are flawless prior to power energization.

Hot Commissioning & Thermal Ramp-Up

Live Fluid Introduction & Closed-Loop Balancing

Controlled Thermal Stabilization

Gradual heat-up of the hydronic reclamation loop at maximum rates of 5°C/hour to prevent thermal shock in heat exchangers and reactor linings.

dT/dt ≤ 5°C / hr
Narrator Insight: Prevents structural material fatigue through controlled thermal gradients.
Process Variable Loop Tuning
  • Dynamic PID loop optimization for pH, flow rate, and pressure control under real fluid viscosity.
  • Verifying anti-surge controls on compressors and main transfer pumps.
  • Testing automated blowdown valve sequencing at designated solute threshold limits.
Narrator Insight: Fine-tunes automation responsiveness under actual operating viscosities.
