Quantitative Risk Rating Index

Prioritizing Action via Risk Severity Evaluation

Risk Matrix Calculation

Determining criticality ratings for high-pressure boilers, chemical loops, and automated valves:

Risk Rating = Probability × Severity × Exposure

Likelihood of occurrence multiplied by impact severity and duration of operational exposure.

Action Thresholds
  • High Risk (15-25): Immediate process shutdown and isolation.
  • Moderate Risk (8-14): Mitigate within 24 hours under active supervisor log.
  • Low Risk (1-7): Routine maintenance scheduling and trend monitoring.
Narrator Insight: Mathematical risk scoring dictates immediate operational response protocols.

Zero Energy State Verification
  • Electrical Isolation: Physical breaker lock and voltage test.
  • Hydraulic/Pneumatic: Bleed-off pressure gauges verified at zero.
  • Chemical Lines: Blind flange insertion and double block-and-bleed.
