Core Mass-Balance Governing Equation

First Law Principles applied to Fluid Loops

Conservation of Mass Principle

In any closed or open control volume, mass cannot be created or destroyed. The rate of change of mass inside the system equals the sum of mass entering minus mass exiting.

ΔMsystem = Σ ˙min - Σ ˙mout

Steady-State Condition: ΔMsystem = 0  &implies;  Σ ˙min = Σ ˙mout

Narrator Insight: Teaches technicians how steady-state mass balance ensures predictable inventory behavior.

Water Contaminant Dynamics

Concentration Cycles & Solute Accumulation

Solute Accumulation Mechanics

As pure water evaporates, dissolved solids remain in circulation. Left unchecked, scaling, pipe fouling, and bio-corrosion accelerate exponentially.

Narrator Insight: Demonstrates why managing dissolved solids prevents infrastructure degradation.
Cycles of Concentration (CoC)

Quantifies how many times a mineral is concentrated relative to raw makeup water:

CoC = [TDS]Blowdown / [TDS]Makeup

Makeup Water Requirement
