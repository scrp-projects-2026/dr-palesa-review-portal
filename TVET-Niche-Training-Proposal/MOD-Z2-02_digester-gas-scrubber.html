CSTR Digester Design & Thermal Balance

Mesophilic Core Operational Kinetics

Core Mechanical Specifications
  • Operating Temperature: Mesophilic target maintained at 37°C (±1.5°C tolerance).
  • Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT): 22 to 28 days for complete COD reduction.
  • Mechanical Agitation: Low-shear, top-entry impeller running at 18 RPM to prevent bacterial floc disruption.
  • Thermal Insulation: Continuous polyurethane jacket with an overall U-value below 0.22 W/m²K.
Thermodynamic Loop Integration

Diverts thermal energy directly from the Co-Gen cooling circuit into inner wall stainless steel heat exchanger coils. Maintains process stability during severe Highveld winter nights down to -6°C without auxiliary electrical resistive heating.

Multi-Stage Gas Scrubbing Architecture

Eliminating Moisture, Hydrogen Sulfide, and Carbon Dioxide

Stage 1: Desiccant Drying

Chilled condensate trap combined with silica gel beds drops raw gas dewpoint to -20°C, removing 99% of entrained water vapor.

Narrator Insight: Prevents acid condensation in piping loops.
Stage 2: H2S Scrubbing

Iron sponge media bed selectively reacts with hydrogen sulfide, lowering concentration from ~2,500 ppm down to <10 ppm.

Narrator Insight: Eliminates sulfur corrosion risks across engine blocks.
Stage 3: CO2 Separation

Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) column strips excess carbon dioxide, boosting net methane concentration to >95% by volume.

Narrator Insight: Upgrades fuel energy density for peak turbine combustion.

Process Automation & Safety Controls

ArIoT Monitoring & Automated Hazard Mitigation

Real-Time pH & VFA Monitoring

Continuous inline probes monitor Volatile Fatty Acid to Alkalinity ratios (FOS/TAC), automatically adjusting feed pump dosing to prevent digester acidification.

Over-Pressure Protection

Dual-stage mechanical pressure relief valves combined with automated emergency flare stacks prevent dome structural damage during sudden gas spikes.

Gas Leakage Detection

Optical infrared CH4 sensors located across scrubber manifolds trigger automated solenoid shut-off valves upon detecting traces >0.5% by volume.
