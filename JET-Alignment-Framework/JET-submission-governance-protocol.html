03. Mandatory Gating Requirements

Pass / Fail Screening Criteria Before Technical Evaluation

Legal & Land Tenure Gating
  • Entity Registration: Applicant must be a fully registered legal entity in good standing.
  • Documented Land Access: Mandatory written leases or title deeds. Unverified government land assumptions lead to immediate disqualification.
Narrator Insight: Zero tolerance for informal or unverified land arrangements.
Market & Community Gating
  • Route to Market: Clear evidence of market uptake or formal off-taker agreements.
  • Community Inclusion: Meaningful participation and economic empowerment of women, youth, and former mine workers.
Narrator Insight: Market viability and social license are mandatory.

04. Statutory Governance: CIPC

Companies and Intellectual Property Commission Requirements

Active Enterprise Status
Ensure the operating entity is registered as 'In Business' with CIPC, with current annual return filings up to date.
Zero dormant entities permitted.
Director Verification
Verify active director identity numbers, residential addresses, and shareholding structures against official CIPC disclosure certificates.
Transparent ownership control.
Memorandum of Incorporation
Confirm that the MOI explicitly accommodates agricultural production, renewable energy, and agro-processing business activities.
Scope alignment check.

05. Statutory Governance: SARS

South African Revenue Service Tax Compliance Status (TCS)

Tax Compliance Status (TCS) Pin
  • Valid TCS Pin: Must generate and submit an active SARS Tax Compliance Status Pin verifying zero outstanding debt or unfiled returns.
  • VAT Registration: Confirm VAT vendor status where projected turnover exceeds the statutory 1 million Rand threshold.
Narrator Insight: Tax non-compliance triggers immediate automated disqualification at intake.
PAYE & SDL Reconciliation
  • Payroll Filings: Ensure EMP201 and EMP501 submissions are fully reconciled for all employed personnel.
  • Good Standing Certificate: Attach official SARS good standing documentation within the application portal vault.
Narrator Insight: Demonstrates corporate fiscal responsibility to funders.

06. Statutory Governance: CSD

National Treasury Central Supplier Database Integration

Supplier Number (MAAA)
Maintain an active CSD registration with a verified MAAA supplier number in good standing across all state spheres.
Mandatory state database entry.
Banking & Ownership Sync
Ensure bank account verification status on CSD matches company registration documents and tax records without discrepancies.
Prevents disbursement delays.
B-BBEE Verification
Upload verified Level 1 to 4 B-BBEE certificates or sworn affidavits to maximize socioeconomic scoring metrics.
Empowerment scoring anchor.
