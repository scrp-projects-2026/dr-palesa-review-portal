05. Broiler & Swine Loading Protocols

Day-1 Placement & Weaner Grouping Standards

Broiler Chick Loading (Day 1)
  • Zonal Isolation: Confine placement within the partitioned 128 m² zone using thick PVC curtains.
  • Hydration Start: Supply warm, glucose-treated water to boost energy reserves.
  • Crop Checks: Physically check chick crop fullness 12 hours post-load to confirm feed access.
Narrator Insight: Day-old chicks cannot self-regulate body heat; minimize transit stress.
Weaner Pig Nursery Onboarding
  • Weight Sorting: Group pigs of uniform size into individual pods (Pods 1 to 4) to eliminate feeding fights.
  • Hygienic Slat Intro: Place piglets directly on elevated virgin plastic slats over concrete flushes.
  • Thermal Check: Verify steam-plumbed floor lines register 32°C at piglet height.
Narrator Insight: Protects arriving 7kg weaners from pathogen introduction and sorting stress.

09. Biosecurity Pods & Gutter Flushes

Airtight Barriers & Hydraulic Waste Operations

4-Pod Isolation System
  • Airtight Barriers: Keep individual entry doors closed tightly to halt airborne pathogen drift.
  • Decoupled Foot Baths: Step into dedicated viral chemical washes before passing doors.
  • Isolated Drainage: Keep pod drain valves shut during targeted observation.
Narrator Insight: Total biosecurity compartmentalization.
Hydraulic Gutter Flush
  • Interval Routine: Execute a full concrete channel flush every 48 hours to maintain hygiene.
  • Sluice Gate Operation: Lift concrete release gates rapidly to utilize maximum hydraulic flushing heads.
  • Gravity Transit: Built-in slopes carry waste slurry directly into continuous digesters without pumps.
Narrator Insight: Zero electrical energy required for waste transport.

10. Biogas & Co-Generation Management

Slurry Balancing, H2S Scrubbing & Heat Extraction

Digester Core Optimization
  • Dilution Ratios: Mix raw swine manure with borehole water at a strict 1:1 fluid weight ratio.
  • Solids Threshold: Maintain overall total volatile solids at an 8% to 10% operational consistency.
  • pH Monitoring: Keep effluent pH stable between 6.8 and 7.4 to prevent souring.
Narrator Insight: Protects anaerobic bacterial health.
Co-Generation & Scrubbing
  • Iron Swarf Traps: Inspect and shake loose reactive binding media every 30 days to bind H2S.
  • Manifold Injection: Route scrubbed methane gas into modified generators at 15-22 kPa.
  • Steam Distribution: Route recovered hot steam through insulated pipe networks into nursery floors.
Narrator Insight: Simultaneous electrical generation and thermal floor heating.
